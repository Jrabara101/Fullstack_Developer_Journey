The Tactician's Rulebook

Objective

Be the first tactician to align four consecutive tokens of your color in any orientation: horizontally across a tier, vertically inside a column, or along either diagonal incline.

Tactile Micro-Interactions

  • Column Previews: Hovering over or focusing on a column displays a suspended preview token above the row, plus a ghost landing guide in the lowest open slot.
  • Gravitational Physics: Pieces fall with accelerated downward velocity and settle with a gentle elastic bounce.
  • Winning Line Sweep: The moment four tokens connect, an incandescent golden beam traces the victory vector.

Accessible Keyboard Controls

← / → or A / D: Shift Column
Enter / Space: Drop Token
1 through 7: Instant Column
Z: Retract (Undo)
R: Instant Rematch
M: Toggle Sound

Dual-Coding & Inclusivity

To eliminate colorblind barriers, tokens are differentiated by both chromatic hue and tactile embossed symbols: Player 1 (Star / Sun) vs Player 2 (Diamond / Rhombus).
