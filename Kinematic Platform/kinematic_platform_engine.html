The Passenger Inheritance Pipeline

1. Player controller adds speed vectors & resolves collisions.
2. Platform moves. Player is left behind or slides off abruptly!
✅ Two-Phase Determinate Execution (Staff Architecture):
Phase A: Update platform vectors first:
   &vec;Dplatform = Pcurrent - Pprev
Phase B: Apply coordinates to locked passenger first:
   Pplayer += &vec;Dplatform
3. Apply standard gravity, horizontal inputs & resolve boundary blocks.
